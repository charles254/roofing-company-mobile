Mobile Bay Coastal Storm Exposure, Wind Uplift & IBHS FORTIFIED Roof™ Standards

Mobile County sits directly within the ASCE 7 wind-borne debris hazard corridor, demanding specialized fastening and moisture barrier techniques. Under IBHS FORTIFIED Roof™ specifications, standard roofing practices are upgraded to eliminate the primary causes of storm-induced water intrusion. Roof sheathing is anchored with 8d ring-shank nails spaced every 4 inches on edges and 6 inches in the field, doubling wind uplift capacity compared to standard smooth staples. Furthermore, the roof deck seams are fully sealed with modified bitumen tape or direct-to-deck peel-and-stick membranes.

Critical Indicators of Storm Distress & Concealed Roof Deck Water Intrusion

Tropical storms, severe convective squalls, and Gulf hurricanes often generate subtle structural compromise that eludes ground inspection. High winds cause shingle tabs to flex and fatigue their factory seal strips, allowing wind-driven moisture to migrate beneath underlayment. Property owners should watch for telltale warning signs across roof slopes, overhangs, and attic structures.

  • Lifted, creased, or missing shingle tabs along perimeter eaves, valleys, and vulnerable ridge caps
  • Damp attic rafters, dark sheathing stains, or wet blown-in insulation after squall lines
  • Accumulated mineral granules in aluminum gutters signaling rapid UV weathering and tab exposure
  • Rusted valley metal, separated step flashing around brick chimneys, or lifted pipe boot collars

Frequently asked questions

What is an IBHS FORTIFIED Roof™ and how does it benefit Mobile AL homeowners?

An IBHS FORTIFIED Roof™ is an engineered roofing standard developed by the Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety. It features ring-shank nails, sealed roof deck seams, and enhanced drip edge fastening to keep wind-driven rain out during hurricanes. In Alabama, a certified FORTIFIED Roof qualifies homeowners for significant insurance premium discounts through AIUA (Alabama Beach Plan) and private carriers.

Can Mobile homeowners use the Strengthen Alabama Homes grant for roof replacement?

Yes. Eligible homeowners in Mobile and Baldwin counties can apply for the Strengthen Alabama Homes grant program, which awards up to $10,000 toward upgrading a primary residence to IBHS FORTIFIED standards.
