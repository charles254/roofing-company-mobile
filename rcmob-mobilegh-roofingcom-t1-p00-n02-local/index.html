Frequently asked questions

Does homeowners insurance cover emergency roof tarping in Mobile?

Yes. Standard homeowner policies require policyholders to take reasonable steps to prevent further damage after a covered loss. Professional emergency tarping and shrink-wrapping costs are typically 100% reimbursable as part of your overall storm damage claim.

What should I do immediately after storm winds damage my roof in Mobile?

First, ensure personal safety by staying clear of sagging ceilings or wet electrical fixtures. Call our 24/7 emergency dispatch line for immediate tarping, document interior and exterior damage with clear photos, and notify your insurance provider to initiate a claim.
